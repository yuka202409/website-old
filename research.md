# Research

My research interests lie at the intersection of applied mathematics, dynamical systems, and mathematical biology.

## Circadian Rhythms

I am interested in mathematical models of biological rhythms, including:

- Circadian oscillators
- Phase reduction
- Phase response curves
- Isochrons
- Isostables
- Phase-amplitude reduction
- Light-dark entrainment

## Mathematical Oncology

I am also interested in mathematical approaches to cancer biology, including:

- Cancer treatment scheduling
- Chronotherapy
- Drug resistance
- Cancer evolution
- Mathematical models of immunotherapy
